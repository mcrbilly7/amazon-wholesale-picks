### Rules for this capital size
1. Start with **2–3 SKUs**, not all 12. The other SKUs are a pipeline for quotes.
2. Prefer **light, non-restricted** Home/Office/Pet-accessory/Sports items (avoid grocery, beauty, supplements unless clearly open).
3. **Never buy** until: (a) Seller Central shows you can list the ASIN, (b) supplier confirms Amazon/online rights in writing, (c) invoice will show your LLC legal name + resale docs.
4. **Faire inventory is not allowed for Amazon** (Faire TOS). Skip Faire-only brands.
5. Run Amazon **Revenue Calculator** on each ASIN before the PO. Low ASP items often lose money after FBA fees.
